# Google Business Profile Optimization Checklist

This document is an owner checklist. It does not update Google automatically.

## Per Branch

- Confirm the exact public business name without keyword stuffing.
- Confirm the map pin, official address, phone, website, and booking URL.
- Confirm normal and special hours, last-client policy, and temporary closures.
- Select the most accurate primary category and only truthful secondary categories.
- Add approved services with accurate descriptions and current approved prices.
- Upload authentic exterior, reception, treatment-room, equipment, and team photographs.
- Remove duplicates and report incorrect salon or hair-service associations.
- Keep doctor availability wording aligned with the website and actual schedule.

## Suggested Category Review

Review options such as aesthetic clinic, skin care clinic, laser hair removal service, and wellness
center against the services actually provided. Do not select dermatologist or dermatology clinic
without verified professional representation.

## Content and Reviews

- Publish one useful, factual update weekly when there is real news or education to share.
- Ask clients for honest reviews without incentives or scripted outcomes.
- Respond without confirming that a reviewer is a patient or exposing treatment information.
- Escalate clinical complaints privately and preserve relevant records.
- Never create fake reviews or guarantee results in posts or responses.

## Production Links

After owner approval, use the canonical production homepage, `/branches`, relevant treatment page,
or branch-preselected appointment link. Do not use local, preview, or temporary deployment URLs.
